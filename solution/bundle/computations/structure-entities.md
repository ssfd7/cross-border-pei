The starting point for a question that names a fund or a company: it gives the identifier the other computations take. An entity with no flag is in the structure in another role, such as the general partner. Investors are not listed here; they are in the investor register.

# Computation

```sql
SELECT e.entity_id AS entity, e.legal_name AS name, e.legal_form,
       e.is_fund = 'Y' AS fund_vehicle, e.is_spv = 'Y' AS spv, e.is_portco = 'Y' AS portfolio_company,
       'entity_mgmt.legal_entity:' || e.entity_id AS source_ref
FROM entity_mgmt.legal_entity e
ORDER BY e.entity_id
```
