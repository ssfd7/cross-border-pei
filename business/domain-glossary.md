# Glossary of acronyms

Acronyms used in the Bocconi *Private Equity and Venture Capital* course, converted from `glossary_of_acronyms.pdf`. The acronym and expansion columns are the course's own. The meaning column is a short explanation drawn from the course slides, which are available to enrolled learners on [Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/learn/private-equity) and are not redistributed in this repository, and the last column says where the term is introduced (`W2 p48` means PDF page 48 of the Week 2 deck).

**Note on "PEI".** The course uses PEI for *private equity investor*. The product names PEI LookThrough and PEI Clearance use it for *private equity investment*.

## Parties and vehicles

| Acronym | Expansion | Meaning | Introduced |
|---|---|---|---|
| AMC | Asset Management Company | The supervised firm that manages one or more funds in the European format | W2 p11–12 |
| CEF | Closed-End Fund(s) | A fund with fixed maturity and fixed size; the main European PE vehicle | W2 p11–13 |
| GPs | General Partners | The managers of a limited partnership, fully liable for its liabilities | W2 p48–49 |
| LLP | Limited Liability Partnership | The form through which a GP management company usually operates | W2 p51 |
| LP | Limited Partnership | The legal entity behind a US or UK fund | W2 p47–48 |
| LPs | Limited Partners | Investors in a limited partnership; they do not manage and are liable only up to their investment | W2 p48–49 |
| PEI | Private Equity Investor(s) | The professional investor buying equity in a non-listed company | W1 p3 |
| SBIC | Small Business Investment Companies | US vehicle created in 1958 and co-owned with a public administration | W2 p56–58 |
| SMEs | Small-Medium Enterprises | The main target of PE investment in Europe | W1 p59 |
| SPACs | Special Purpose Acquisition Companies | A listed shell that raises money to buy a single company | W2 p83 |
| SPV | Special Purpose Vehicle | An "empty box" entity created for one transaction | W1 p36 |
| VBC | Venture-Backed Company | The company financed by the PE investor; the portfolio company | W1 p4 |
| VCF | Venture Capital Funds | The most common US and UK PE vehicle, organised as a limited partnership | W2 p47, p64 |
| VCT | Venture Capital Trusts | UK trust-based vehicle open to retail investors | W2 p65–68 |

## Deals and financing

| Acronym | Expansion | Meaning | Introduced |
|---|---|---|---|
| CG deals | Corporate Governance Deals | Investment made to redesign a company's governance, often around succession | W1 p46 |
| IPO | Initial Public Offering | Listing on a stock exchange; an alternative source of finance and an exit route | W1 p3; W3 p62 |
| LBO | Leverage Buyout | Acquisition of a target through a highly indebted SPV | W1 p42–44 |
| M&A | Merger(s) and Acquisition(s) | Growth by acquiring or merging with another company | W1 p33 |
| PE | Private Equity | Investment in the equity of a non-listed company; includes venture capital | W1 p3 |
| PIPE | Private Investment in Public Equity | A PE-style minority investment in a listed company | W1 p45 |
| PPP | Public-Private Partnerships | Joint public and private investment vehicles, such as SBICs | W2 p58, p69 |
| VC | Venture Capital | PE in the early stages of a company's life: seed, startup, early growth | W1 p3, p17 |

## Regulation and tax

| Acronym | Expansion | Meaning | Introduced |
|---|---|---|---|
| AIFM | Alternative Investment Fund Managers | The EU directive regulating managers of closed-end funds | W2 p6 |
| EVCA | European Venture Capital Association | European industry association | W1 p56 |
| LPA | Limited Partnership Agreement | The contract between GPs and LPs that governs the fund | W2 p50 |
| PEX | Participation Exemption | Reduced tax on a vehicle's capital gains when conditions are met | W2 p73 |
| QSBS | Qualified Small Business Stock | US rule the slides describe as relieving tax on gains reinvested in another private company | W2 p60 |

## Valuation and returns

| Acronym | Expansion | Meaning | Introduced |
|---|---|---|---|
| CFt | Cash Flow at time "t" | The cash flow of a given year in a DCF valuation | W4 p8, p15 |
| D/E | Debt-to-Equity | Ratio of debt to equity in a company's capital structure | W2 p77; W4 p20 |
| DCF | Discounted Cash Flow | Valuation as the present value of future cash flows | W4 p7 |
| EBIT | Earnings Before Interest and Taxes | Operating profit | W4 p14 |
| EBITDA | Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation and Amortization | Gross operating margin | W4 p14 |
| EBT | Earnings Before Taxes | Profit after interest and before income tax | W4 p14 |
| EV | Enterprise Value | Value of the whole business before deducting net debt and minorities | W4 p8–10 |
| IRR | Internal Rate of Return | The discount rate equating an investment with the present value of its returns | W2 p31 |
| NFP | Net Financial Position | Net debt deducted from enterprise value to reach equity value | W4 p8, p23 |
| SA | Surplus (non-operating) Assets | Assets not needed for operations, added in the equity value formula | W4 p8 |
| TV | Terminal Value | Value of cash flows beyond the forecast period | W4 p22 |
| VCM | Venture Capital Method | Valuation method linking expected IRR to the share of equity to acquire | W4 p45–46 |
| WACC | Weighted Average Cost of Capital | Blended cost of debt and equity used as the DCF discount rate | W4 p16–21 |
